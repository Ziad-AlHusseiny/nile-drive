# Nile Drive: owner notes

## The idea

A small car rental service for people who visit Egypt for one, two, or three months. The first group is people who live in the Gulf and come to Egypt to see family or work.

Start with a small area: Cairo and Giza. Add more places only when the team can serve them well.

The main message is: **One car. Your whole stay.**

## Six useful service ideas

1. **30, 60, or 90 days.** Show a clear price for each stay. Let the owner set a lower price for longer stays.
2. **Airport or home pickup.** Ask where the guest wants the car. Show any delivery fee in the quote.
3. **Plan before the flight.** Ask for the dates and car choice before the guest comes to Egypt. Confirm the car and pickup plan in writing.
4. **A car for each trip.** Use simple groups: a sedan for city trips, or an SUV for family trips. Show seats and bag space.
5. **Ask for more time.** Let guests ask to keep the car longer. Confirm this only when the car is free.
6. **Family extras.** Let guests ask for a child seat or a second driver. Confirm the price and terms before pickup.

For a small fleet, clear answers and good personal service matter. Show real car photos. Use a shared car check at pickup and return. Do not promise a service the team cannot provide.

## What the website does now

- Shows a sample fleet and sample prices.
- Lets a visitor choose a car, date, stay length, and pickup place.
- Builds a rental request that the visitor can copy or download.
- Does not send a request to the owner.
- Does not confirm a booking or take payment.

Prices, offers, mileage, and service options are examples. The owner must check them before real use. Car images are for the design and must be replaced with real fleet photos before launch.

## Owner details needed before real rentals

| Item | Details needed |
| --- | --- |
| Brand | Final name, logo, business address, and working hours. |
| Contact | The real phone number, WhatsApp number, and email. Who answers requests, and when? |
| Fleet | Real make, model, year, photos, seats, bag space, fuel type, and gearbox for each car. |
| Car dates | A clear record of when each car is free. The team must check it before a booking is confirmed. |
| Prices | Real 30, 60, and 90-day prices; included distance; extra-distance price; tax; and all extra fees. |
| Deposit | Amount, payment method, return time, and reasons for any charge. |
| Driver details | The exact ID and licence rules the business will use. Have these rules checked by a local expert. |
| Rental terms | Payment, cancellation, late return, fuel, damage, cover, extra drivers, and travel limits. Have the terms checked before use. |
| Pickup | Places served, delivery fees, pickup hours, and the airport meeting plan. |
| Support | Who helps if the car has a problem, and what help the business can provide. |
| Requests | Where new requests will go, who checks them, and how the team sends a written quote. |
| Guest data | What data the team needs, who can see it, and when it will be deleted. |

Keep the website as a request tool until these details are ready. A guest should get a clear quote and written booking confirmation from the team.
